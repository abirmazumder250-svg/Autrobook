Unique Features (Not on Facebook)

These features make Autrobook truly unique and user-focused.

  • 18+ Content Guard: Global blur/hide for all mature content (Facebook relies on community standards).
  • Zen Mode: Focus mode to hide all distractions (comments, likes) for mindful scrolling.
  • AI Post Coach: Real-time tone, grammar, and clarity checker before you post.
  • Digital Detox Timer: Lock the app for a set time to take a break from social media.
  • Voice Comment Effects: Normal, Eco, and Deep voice presets for audio comments.
  • Profile Pic Guard: Advanced blur for profile pictures to protect identity from strangers.
  • Live Viral Insights: See a real-time percentage of how viral your post is going.
  • Time Capsule Posts: Schedule posts to unlock at a specific future date/time.
  • Anti-Toxic AI Filter: User-facing toggle to auto-hide toxic/hateful comments.
  • Trust Network: See who your friends truly trust based on mutual interactions.
  • Comfort Mode: Reduce animations, soften colors, and dim the screen for eye comfort.
  • Anti-Screenshot Guard: Block screenshots on your private posts.
  • Carbon Tracker: Track the CO2 footprint of your scrolling and earn eco-badges.
  • Sleep Sync: App dims and locks automatically based on your sleep schedule.
  • Focus Playlist: Auto-play Lo-Fi or classical music when you open the feed.
  • AI Friend Match: Smart suggestions based on shared values, not just mutual friends.
  • Ghost Profile: Browse profiles without leaving a "seen" trace.
  • Blood Donation Hub: Hyper-local, NID-verified blood matching (Donor/Receiver).
  • Lower Data Mode: Force SD videos/images to save mobile data.
  • Biometric Lock: Require fingerprint or FaceID every time the app opens.
  • Offline Drafts: Write posts offline and auto-publish when you reconnect.
  • Smart Albums: AI automatically groups your photos into events and trips.
  • Live Polls: Add interactive polls to posts and see live results.
  • Ghost Mode: Appear offline while still browsing the feed.
  • Data Download: Download all your data securely with one click.
  • Legacy Contact: Assign someone to manage your account after you pass away.
  • Memorial Mode: Turn a profile into a memorial page to remember loved ones.
  • Built-in VPN: Browse securely with a built-in VPN toggle.
  • Ad Blocker & Tracker Blocker: Block third-party ads and trackers.
  • 3D Avatars & Sticker Maker: Create custom 3D avatars and sticker packs.
