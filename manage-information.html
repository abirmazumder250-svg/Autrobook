3. Clearing & Managing Stored Data

Clear & Delete

You can reset locally stored Autrobook preference keys in this browser or clear site data directly from your browser settings.

What Clearing Local Keys Does Instant
  • Resets saved cookie consent choices, subscription selection state, and reviewed standard markers on this device.
