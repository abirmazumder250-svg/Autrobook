1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website or browser. Alongside cookies, modern web applications use HTML5 local storage, session storage, and device preference keys to remember your state across page reloads and visits.

  • Session storage / cookies: Cleared when you close your browser tab or session.
  • Persistent storage / cookies: Stored on your device for a set period or until cleared manually so preferences like consent persist.
  • First-party vs. third-party: Set directly by Autrobook or by integrated services used to deliver features.

5. Controlling Cookies in Your Browser

In addition to the controls above, most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies and site data through browser privacy settings. Blocking all cookies or site storage may affect saved preferences and page behavior.

  • Clearing site data in your browser will reset locally stored preferences.
  • Private or incognito browsing modes typically clear session data once the window closes.
