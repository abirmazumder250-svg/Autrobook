Autrobook Community Standards

Core Values
Platform: Autrobook 18+ Free Updated 2026

Our Community Standards outline what is and is not allowed across posts, stories, reels, pages, comments, and interactions on Autrobook. These rules exist to keep expression open while protecting safety, authenticity, and respect across the community.

1. Safety & Harm Prevention

Safety & Harm

We do not allow content or activity that threatens real-world safety, promotes severe harm, or encourages dangerous behavior.

Prohibited Activity
  • Credible threats of violence, physical harm, or incitement to violence.
  • Promotion, instruction, or encouragement of self-harm or dangerous challenges.
  • Distribution of non-consensual explicit media or exploitative content.

2. Respect, Bullying & Hateful Conduct

Respect & Conduct

Healthy communities rely on mutual dignity. Targeted harassment, intimidation, and attacks against protected groups are not permitted.

What We Remove
  • Targeted harassment, dogpiling, or sharing private personal contact details without consent.
  • Dehumanizing language, slurs, or incitement to hatred based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or disability.
  • Repeated unwanted contact after a user blocks or restricts an account.

3. Authenticity & Account Integrity

Authenticity & Spam

People should be able to trust who they are connecting with and know that accounts and engagement are genuine.

Integrity Guidelines
  • Do not impersonate another person, creator, organization, or brand to deceive others.
  • Avoid coordinated inauthentic behavior, fake engagement loops, or automated manipulation.
  • Keep profile details accurate and respect account security boundaries.

4. Spam, Scams & Deceptive Practices

Authenticity & Spam

We work to keep feeds clear of disruptive bulk messaging, financial scams, phishing links, and deceptive schemes.

Restricted Practices
  • Repetitive unsolicited promotions, clickbait traps, or misleading external redirects.
  • Fraudulent giveaways, credential harvesting links, or impersonation scams.
  • Manipulative media intended to mislead people about critical events or safety notices.

5. Intellectual Property & Privacy

Respect & Conduct

Creators should respect the rights, original work, and privacy of others when sharing text, images, audio, and video.

Creator Responsibility
  • Only share content you created or have permission/rights to distribute.
  • Respect copyright, trademark, and attribution expectations.
  • Do not expose private documents, government IDs, or non-public personal records.

6. Reporting, Enforcement & Appeals

Reporting & Appeals

When violations occur, enforcement actions may include content removal, reach limits, feature restrictions, or account suspension depending on severity and history.

How Review Works
  • Users can report content or accounts that appear to violate these standards.
  • Repeated or severe violations carry stronger enforcement measures.
  • If you believe a decision was made in error, you can request a review through available support channels.
