Who can suggest your profile based on your phone number or email address?

If someone has your phone number or email address, you can choose if you want to be suggested to them based on that information.

Do you want search engines outside of Autrobook to link to your profile? When this setting is on, search engines may link to your profile in their results.

When this setting is off, search engines will stop linking to your profile, but this may take some time. Your profile can still be found if people search for your name.

How you get message requests

Not all messages are requests. Messages like those from friends and groups go to your Chats. See the full list of who can message you.
