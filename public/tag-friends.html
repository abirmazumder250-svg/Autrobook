Collaborative posts

Sending a collaboration invite

  • If your collaborator accepts, their name will be added to your post and it will be shared on their Page or profile.
  • Your collaborator will be able to see aggregated insights for this content in Autro Business Suite.
  • If your collaborative post is eligible for monetization, earnings will be available only to the primary creator.
  • If your collaborator removes themselves from the post, their name will be removed from your post.
  • You can remove your collaborator anytime. This will remove their name from your post and your post won’t be shared on their Page or profile.
