How to Use Autrobook Features

Explore clear, point-by-point instructions for every core tool on Autrobook—from mindful feed pacing and privacy controls to fact-checking and Autro AI Assistant help.

Step-by-Step Feature Guides

1. Autro AI Assistant (Smart Help Companion)

Open AI Assistant

  • Step-by-Step Walkthroughs: Request clear instructions for muting noisy threads, scheduling calm digests, or checking claims without digging through menus.
  • Quick Summaries & Explanations: Paste long notes or questions to get concise, structured answers tailored to your workflow.
  • Everyday Productivity: Use Autro AI Assistant to brainstorm ideas, organize catch-up notes, or find the right page inside Autrobook quickly.

2. Calm Digest Mode & Mindful Feed Controls

Open Calm Digest
  • Group Catch-Ups by Time Slot: Open Calm Digest Mode (calm-digest.html), add a topic and summary note, and assign it to Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night.
  • Track Reading Calmly: Filter items by slot or Unread status, mark completed items as Read, or mark all as read in one tap.
  • Pace Your Scrolling: Pair Calm Digest Mode with Slow-Scroll Feed (slow-scroll-feed.html) and Chronological Purity (chronological-purity.html) for an unhurried, time-ordered view.
  • Set Healthy Boundaries: Use Digital Sunset Schedule (digital-sunset.html) and Anti-Doomscroll Nudge (anti-doomscroll.html) to wind down screen time naturally.

3. Thread Mute & Snooze + Visibility Controls

Open Thread Mute
  • Pause Noisy Discussions: Go to Thread Mute & Snooze (thread-mute-snooze.html), enter a thread or conversation name, choose Snooze or Mute, and pick a duration from 1 Hour up to 7 Days.
  • Manage Active & Expired Rules: Monitor remaining time live, filter by Active, Snooze, Mute, or Expired, re-snooze with one click, or unmute anytime.
  • Protect Your Presence: Enable Ghost Mode (ghost-mode.html) for quiet browsing and organize sharing groups with Circle Tiers (circle-tiers.html).
  • Ephemeral & Memory Tools: Send temporary reminders via Burn-After-Read Notes (burn-after-read-notes.html) and revisit highlights with Rewind Capsule (rewind-capsule.html).

4. Fact-Check Lens & Content Safety Tools

Open Fact-Check Lens
  • Scan Claims & Headlines: Open Fact-Check Lens (fact-check-lens.html) and paste a claim, headline, or statistic to see sensational wording cues and source context signals.
  • Log Verification Notes: Assign a review status (Context Needed, Verified, Unverified, or Disputed), attach source references and context notes, and search or filter your log later.
  • Preview Links & Shield Spoilers: Inspect destination links safely with Link Peeker (link-peeker.html) and hide plot twists using Spoiler Shield (spoiler-shield.html).
  • Filter Low-Signal Posts & Check Reach: Reduce repetitive noise with AI Slop Filter (ai-slop-filter.html) and review post visibility using Shadowban & Reach Check (reach-check.html).

5. Profile, Voice Bio & Avatar Stickers Studio

Open Profile
  • Personalize Your Identity: Visit Profile (profile.html) to update your public details and expression settings.
  • Add Audio & Visual Flair: Record short audio introductions in Voice Bio & Echoes (voice-bio-echoes.html) and craft custom avatars or stickers in Avatar & Stickers Studio (avatar-stickers.html).
  • Draft Offline Safely: Save unfinished ideas in Offline Draft Vault (offline-draft-vault.html) so your work stays accessible even with spotty connectivity.

6. Settings Search & Theme Comfort

Open Settings
  • Find Settings Fast: Use the search bar at the top of Settings & privacy (settings.html) to filter tools by name or keyword instantly.
  • Automatic Light & Dark Modes: Pages automatically adapt between crisp light mode and dark mode to match your system display preference.
  • Easy Back Navigation: Use the top-left back arrow on any feature page to return smoothly to Settings or Profile.
