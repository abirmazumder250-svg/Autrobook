Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026  •  Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Autrobook collects when you use our platform, how we use and protect that information, and the choices you have over your account and content.

1. What We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide, maintain, and personalize your Autrobook experience:

  • Account & Sign-In Details: When you sign in (including with Google or email verification), we receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile picture to create and personalize your profile.
  • Profile & Content You Share: Information you choose to add or publish—such as your bio, avatar stickers, posts, stories, reels, comments, voice clips, or messages—is stored so your account and features work as intended.
  • Technical & Reliability Data: Basic technical details (such as browser type, device screen preferences, session state, and error logs) help us keep pages responsive, prevent abuse, and fix bugs.

2. How We Use It

We use your information responsibly and strictly for core platform operations:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain your Autrobook account and session.
  • Display your profile, posts, stories, and interactions according to your selected audience and privacy settings.
  • Support requested features such as interface customization, theme preferences, and account notifications.
  • Protect users against spam, unauthorized access, and policy violations.
Our Promise: We never sell your personal information or profile data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. Sharing & Visibility

Your content visibility follows the audience options you choose when posting or updating your profile:

  • Audience Settings: Content shared as public, with friends, or with specific people is shown according to those privacy choices.
  • Service Infrastructure: We rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting, authentication, and database services) solely to run Autrobook securely on our behalf.
  • Legal & Safety Requirements: We may disclose information only when reasonably required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect the safety and rights of users and the platform.

4. Cookies & Storage

We use browser storage technologies—including essential cookies, local storage, and session storage—for practical, functional purposes:

  • Keep you signed in securely across page visits and refreshes.
  • Remember your display preferences, layout choices, and temporary draft states.
  • Ensure smooth navigation and reliable feature performance.

We do not use invasive cross-site ad-tracking cookies to follow you around the web.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and access-control safeguards designed to protect your account and data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. While no internet service can guarantee 100% absolute security, we continually review and improve our protections and encourage you to keep your account credentials safe.

6. Your Rights & Controls

You stay in charge of your presence on Autrobook:

  • Update or edit your profile details, picture, and bio from your profile or settings.
  • Review, edit, archive, or delete content you have created where supported by your account controls.
  • Adjust privacy preferences or reach out to us if you need help managing or removing your account data.

7. Retention & Deletion

We retain account and content data for as long as needed to provide your Autrobook account and features, or as required for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Temporary items (such as short-lived stories or cached drafts) may expire or be cleared automatically, and deleted content may take time to purge from active systems and backups.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or privacy requests regarding this policy or your Autrobook account, please contact us anytime at autrobooksupport@gmail.com.
